= Mutating

The susceptibility of personas to mutation is affected by xref:mutations:a_introduction.adoc#_mutations_and_families[family,window=_blank] and choice.
The families have varying frequencies of mutation based on tropes from popular fiction and the ability of the species to adapt to mutation.
The players have input as to whether their persona mutates or not.
If the player desires to mutate, she can double her persona’s chance of mutation.
This desire can be a source of backstory like the persona went skinny dipping in that glowing creek.

== Acquisition
Most mutations are doled out during persona generation.
These are called startup mutations and the player can choose to mutate or not mutate.
Each family and genus has different parameters for mutation acquisition.
Mutations acquired through game play are rare.
These are called de novo mutations.
De novo mutations came from radioactive misadventures and extreme pharma.

=== Startup
These are mutations that the persona acquires while being generated.
The frequency of start-up mutations depends on the persona family and type.
For anthros, the player can influence the mutation chance by choice.

Defective mutations do not count as a mutation for player personas, and an additional non defect mutation should be rolled.

=== De Novo
These are new mutations or mutation effects acquired during gameplay.
New mutations are rare during a campaign.
Pharma that generates mutations is rare.
Exposure to toxins and radiation is more likely to be lethal than mutagenic.
Adding de novo mutations should be a carefully integrated part of the milieu or campaign.
