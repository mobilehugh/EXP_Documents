= Anthro Size

Persona size has two components, hite and wate.
Hite, aka height, is the persona’s length in centimetres (cm).
Wate, aka weight, is the persona’s volume/mass in kilograms (kg).
Anthros are human-sized.
In the vernacular of EXP, anthros are *medium sized*.
The occasional ursidae may sneak onto the bottom of the large scale.

Hite and wate are genderless in EXP.
The player can choose a descriptor to influence their hite and wate.
The player is also welcome to choose a hite and wate that suits their persona.
Don’t let rules get in the way of the story or fun.

== Hite
Anthro persona hite is measured in centimeters (cm).
The player is welcome to choose whatever hite she desires as long as it fits into the milieu and story.
For those who wish to generate their persona's hite you are in the correct place.

Each anthro genus has a hite calculation listed with their persona.
A canine anthro has a hite calculation of 150+(1d12-8).
The player rolls 1d12 subtracts 8 and then adds that value to 150.
If the player rolls 8, their canine's hite would be 150 cm.

.*Hite Determination*
* Canine hite calculation is 150+(1d12-8)
* if 1d12 rolls 12, 150 + (4) = 154 cm
* if 1d12 rolls 1, 150 + (-7) = 143 cm

Calculating hite is that simple.
First number is the base hite of the anthro type.
The base hite for a canine is 150 cm.
This number becomes important when calculating the persona's wate.

== Wate
Anthro persona wate is measured in kilograms (kg).
The player is welcome to choose whatever wate she desires as long as it fits into the milieu and story.
For those who wish to generate their persona's wate you are in the correct place.

Each anthro has a wate value.
This number is called the base wate.
The persona's wate is calculated.
There are no dice rolls made.
The personas hite and base hite are used to calculate the persona's wate

Our canine friend above has a base hite of 150.
This is the first number in the hite calculation *150*+(1d12-8)
The canine also has a base wate value of 48.

Every persona rolls a variance of 2d20-20.
This is used to massage the hite and push the wate into a wider range.
There is no need to understand this, but it will be more fun if you use the variance.

.*Wate Determination*
. Determine the base hite
.. Canine base hite is 150
. Determine the variance (2d20-20)
.. for example 20
. Add the actual hite and variance
.. 154+20 is 174
. Divide the base hite by the variance
. 150 / 174 = 0.86
. Determine the base wate
.. Canine base wate is 48
. Modify the base wate by the fraction
.. 48 * 0.86 = 41 kg

Often arithmetic averse players just use the base wate.
