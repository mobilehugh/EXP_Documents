Humanoids is the common name for the Hominidae Homo Mutabilis.
Humanoids can appear as any hominid type.
Humanoids can appear as simians (monkeys, apes), suidae (pigs, boars), elves, dwarfs, orcs, human beings etc.
Humanoids resembling humans can sport any colour: white, black, red, yellow, green, blue, etc.
None of these humanoid types have any of their mythical abilities.
Humanoids that resemble elves or leprechauns would have none of their mythical abilities.

Example species: Chimp, Dwarf, Elf, Gorilla, Human, Neanderthal, Orc, Pig.

== Mutability
Homo mutabilis has no abilities other than their extreme susceptibility to mutation.
If a player wants her humanoid persona to have mutations, she automatically gets mutations.
If a player does not want her humanoid persona to have mutations, she will likely get them anyway.
