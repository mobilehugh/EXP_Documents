= Anthro Name

The player must name her persona.
Every persona must have a https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/moniker[moniker], call sign, label, title, handle or cognomen.
A player should avoid naming her persona with her mundane world real name.
Creating a persona name makes role-playing more fun.

== Moniker
A moniker is the name the player chooses for her persona.
Sometimes the persona name is obvious and requires no effort.
Other times the player needs some help coming up with a moniker.
The anthro’s vocation, genus, and species are the most potent name determiners.

Each anthro will usually have a given name and a surname.
However there are no set rules.
It would be good to choose a name that matches the milieu and story.
Anthro names need not be silly.

== Taxonomy
Anthros are part of the faux taxonomy.
An anthro's family is Hominidae.
There genus is determined by their anthro type.
So a rodentia anthro is Homo Rodens.
The species is usually determined by the type of rodent anthropomorphized.
If the persona is a rat their full faux scientific name could be Homo Rodens Ratti.

== Common
The common name is what the species calls itself, or how it is identified by others.
On mundane terra we do not herd bos taurus we herd cows.
The common name for anthros is usually just anthro, folk, or people.
Common names for rodens rattus could be Ratfolk, Tailin or just Rats.
The common name for a wide population of anthros will usually require input from the referee, if not the entire table.

== Examples

.*Blue shelled snapping turtle mechanic*
. Moniker: Hans Tinker
. Taxonomy: Homo Serpens Snappus
. Common: Reptilian or Shellian

.*Sad-looking, dark grey hound dog spie with unkempt clothes*
. Moniker: Hans Ophmistuph
. Taxonomy: Homo Canis Droopius
. Common: Canine

.*Pink haired, elven featured humanoid nothing wearing bangles*
. Moniker: Clara Voyant
. Taxonomy: Homo Mutabilis Elfinus
. Common: Humanoid, Elven
