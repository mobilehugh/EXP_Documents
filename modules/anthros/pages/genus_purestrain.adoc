Purestrain is the common name for the Hominidae Homo Sapiens.
Purestrain humans are the collection of mammals control mundane terra.
You are a purestrain human.
The term purestrain refers to their lack of fun Sciency Fiction mutations or diseases of any sort.
Purestrains can choose from any skin colour: black, blue, white, green, yellow, red, gold, etc.
Purestrains possess none of the mythical strengths or weaknesses from unpopular culture.

There is a small chance a purestrain may catch a psionic mutation.
If the player does not want her persona to mutate, a purestrain human persona will not mutate.
If the persona does mutate, she cannot have a defect or a mutation that alters her appearance.

Example species: Black, Blue, Brown, Green, Red, Tan, White, Yellow.
