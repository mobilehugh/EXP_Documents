= Anthro Attributes

Anthro attributes are generated randomly and added to the persona record sheet.
Attributes determine both the physical abilities and type of the anthro persona.
Randomly determining anthro attributes is based on ruleset legacy and game balance.
Using multiple dice to generate random numbers creates more organic ability curves.
The weak and strong are outliers.

== Mind Adjustment
The Mind attribute of an anthro persona increases with her Intelligence attribute.
This Mind adjustment is only for anthro personas.
Most referees will not reduce a persona’s Mind during persona creation.
The intelligence adjustment of Mind can lead to a persona with a negative attribute.
Attributes less than one is fatal for personas.

== Hit Points
Hit points (HPS) measure how much physical punishment a persona can endure before going unconscious or dying.
An anthro’s Constitution determines her physiologic health, and in turn her hit points.
Hit points are a dynamic currency of well being.
They are reduced when the anthro is injured, and losing too many hit points is fatal.

For anthros hit points also determines consciousness and ability to recover from being stunned.

=== Hit Points Max (HPM)
*aka full health*

When a player is generating her anthro's hit points for the first time she is creating her personas hit point max.
The hit points max (HPM) is the full health for the persona.
The persona gets one hit point per point of constitution (CON) plus 1d8 per two points of constitution.
So a persona with a CON of 10 would get 10 plus 5d8 hit points.
HPM is generated once at anthro generation and is recorded like any other attribute.
A good HPM is required to become a mercenary.

.*Hit Points Max (HPM) Full Health*
* *HPM = (1/2 CON x 1d8) + CON*
* Rounding benefits the player
** CON = 5, 3d8 + 5
** CON = 12, 6d8 + 12


=== Minimum Hit Points
*aka death*

Hit points is an attribute that can drop below one without killing the persona.
A persona with a negative hit point total is unconscious and dying from physical trauma.
Having a negative hit point total is often referred to as “being negative.”
A persona with a negative hit points value is unconscious, and can do nothing other than lay about and bleed.

As an anthro gets damaged in combat her hit points keep going down.
At zero hit points an anthro goes unconscious and is dying.
Hit points can be returned by healing, and dying can be stopped by resuscitation.
These interventions are described in the vocation xref:vocations:veterinarian.adoc[veterinarian].

.*Minimum Hit Points (Death)*
* *Unconscious = Zero Hit Points*
** Unconscious personas cannot act
* *Death = Zero - (1/2 HPM)*
** HPS immediately start draining once negative
** HPM = 22, dead at -11 HPS
** HPM = 40, dead at -20 HPS

The persona loses one hit point every five units (10 seconds) her hit point total is negative.
A persona at -2 hit points will drop to -3 hit points five units later, and then -4 hits points 10 seconds after that.
This physiologic collapse continues until the hit point total reaches her hit point minimum, and the persona is dead.
This rapid decline to death does not stop unless medically treated.
For more information about healing and death jump to xref:uncombat:health.adoc[Health,window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"].

=== System Shock
*optional rule for getting stunned in combat*

System shock checks to see if a persona is incapacitated by a bolus of damage.
Whenever a persona loses more than half her hit point total (HPS) in one attack, she must make a System Shock roll, or fall unconscious.
Unlike the unconsciousness described in hit points minimum the persona is not dying.
This is like a stun check.
This rule is optional and hardly ever used.

.*Damage System Shock Roll*
* *1d20 + HPS + CON + Level > 42*
* the player must score higher than 42 or be stunned
* HPS is present hit points not HPM, CON is constitution
** HPS = 10, CON = 10, Level = 3, 1d20 = 10; 33 < 42 -> knocked out
** HPS = 10, CON = 10, Level = 3, 1d20 = 20; 43 > 42 -> keep fighting


=== Recovery
*optional rule for recovering from a combat stun*

A recovery roll (REC) is a check to see if a persona awakens from their trauma-induced slumber.
The persona remains inactive until she makes a successful recovery roll.
The REC is a specialized attribute roll that takes into consideration the persona’s hit point total.

.*Recovery Roll*
* *1d20 + HPS + CON + Level > 32*
* the player must score higher than 32 or remain stunned
* HPS is present hit points not HPM, CON is constitution
** HPS = 10, CON = 10, Level = 3, 1d20 = 5; 28 < 32 -> stay down
** HPS = 10, CON = 10, Level = 3, 1d20 = 20; 43 > 32 -> back in the fight

Recovery rolls only apply to personas with an HPS greater than 0.
The player can check every few units during combat or whenever the referee feels a recovery roll is appropriate.
Once combat has ended, all stunned personas should immediately recover and join in on the game.
