.*Task Descriptions*
* *Bake A Pie*: Make a pie from ingredients.
* *Broken*: Is it broken? Yes/No.
* *Compactify*: Decrease wate by 2 times.
* *Fabricate*: Create a new toy.
* *Find Data*: Get needed data from data stream.
* *Hard Repair*: Fully repair less than Critical damage.
* *ID Alien Artifact*: Identify alien or tech level 20 plus artifact.
* *ID High Tech Artifact*: Identify toy tech level 15 to 19.
* *ID Low Tech Artifact*: Identify toy tech level less than 10.
* *ID Normal Artifact*: Identify toy tech level 10.
* *Mechanical*: Is it a machine? Yes/No.
* *Miniaturization*: Decrease wate by 10 times.
* *Mnemonic Trapping*: Memorize a technical thing.
* *Multi-equip*: Integrate two toys into one.
* *Quick Repair*: Unbreak for one more use. Trivial and minor damage.
* *Repair*: Fully repair less than major damage.
* *Reverse Polarity*: Make a machine go backwards. Once.
* *Robot Command*: Impair a robot with words and sounds
* *Robot Control*: Take over a robot.
* *Robot Override*: Temporarily control a robot.
* *Scavenge Parts*: Get some good parts from a machine.
* *Sotto to Flotto*: Move a gun to full auto.
* *Terminal Access*: Access a computer.
* *Tough Repair*: Fully repair less than Cataclysmic damage.
