.*Task Descriptions*
* *Deflect Incoming*: Deflect an energy shoot attack (lazer, plasma) with her energy sword.
* *Deflect With Palm*: Deflect an energy shoot attack (lazer, plasma) with her open palm.
* *Disarm, Energy Sword*: Amputate the weapon hand from an attacker in close combat. Requires strike attack roll.
* *Disarm, Telekinesis*: Attract an opponent’s pistol or sword immediately into palm. Range = nearby.
* *False Rest*: Appear asleep. Use Kirlian site with eyes closed.
* *Imbue Energy Sword*: Declare a generic energy sword the knite’s bespoke energy sword.
* *Imbue Talisman*: Turn a non-technological trinket or knick-knack into a Kirlian Talisman.
* *Kirlian Danger*: Detect bad intent Kirlian aura.
* *Kirlian Defence*: Double MND when defending against psionic intrusion via the think space.
* *Kirlian Fate*: See general fate of Kirlian aura.
* *Kirlian Find*: Find hidden objects or personas.
* *Kirlian Intent*: See general intent of Kirlian aura.
* *Kirlian Sight*: See biological outlines without light.
* *Kirlian Truth*: See truthfulness of Kirlian aura.
* *Living*: Is it biological and living? Yes/No.
* *Mimic*: Create benign sound to distract target.
* *Muffle*: Induce silence over a quiet sound.
* *Multiple Attacks*: One attack per gift when using energy sword.
* *Pressure*: Psionically incapacitate a persona with choking fear or guilt. Range = nearby.
* *Reflect Incoming*: Return an energy shoot attack (lazer, plasma) with her energy sword. Raw Shoot Attack.
* *Suggestion, Spoken*: Get persona to repeat what the knite just spoke.
* *Suggestion, Thought*: Get persona to repeat what the knite just thought.
* *Telekinesis, Drama*: Attract a light (<1 kg) unfastened object immediately into palm. Range = MND hexes.
* *Telekinesis, Fling*: Turn random objects into fling attacks. 1d4 DMG per gift, no force bonus.
* *Telekinesis, Massive*: Slowly and dramatically lift something stupidly big. Like a 5000 kg vehicle. Range = nearby.
