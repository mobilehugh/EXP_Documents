.*Task Descriptions*
* *Admin Pharma*: No adverse effects.
* *Aging Stagnation*: Arrest aging effect. One at a time.
* *Attribute Boost*: Temporarily boost attribute 1d3 for minutes.
* *Attribute Enhance*: Permanently increase an attribute by 1 (not MIND).
* *Autopsy, Diagnostic*: Disassemble to determine disease death.
* *Autopsy, Forensic*: Disassemble to determine criminal death.
* *Autopsy, Fun*: Disassemble a cadaver.
* *Biorganic Attachment*: Attach a biorganic attachment.
* *Biorganic Replacement*: Insert a biorganic replacement.
* *Biological*: Is it biological? Yes/No.
* *Calm Frenzy*: Arrest an ursidae frenzy.
* *Calm Persona*: Help a persona relax.
* *Cryogenic Sleep*: Safely place into cryogenic sleep.
* *Cryogenic Wake*: Safely emerge from cryogenic sleep.
* *Death Pinch*: Touch target for cardiac arrest (anthro only).
* *Fail Pharma*: Guarantee adverse effects.
* *Heal 50*: Restore to 50% HPM. Uncombat only.
* *Heal 90*: Restore to 90% HPM. Uncombat only.
* *Hearing Restoration*: Reverse any deafness.
* *Mind Inventory*: Detect mind control.
* *Open Stasis Bag*: Retrieve patient from stasis bag alive.
* *Paralyzing Pinch*: Touch target for physical collapse (anthro only).
* *Phenomic Removal*: Safely remove a phenomic mutation.
* *Pharma Creation*: I wanna new drug.
* *Poison Arrest*: Stop poison progress. Not heal.
* *Poison Cure*: Reverse all poison effects (even HPS damage).
* *Poison Reverse*: Reverse the effects of a poison (except HPS damage).
* *Power Fix*: Heal 3d6 HPS over 3 units.
* *Psionic Break*: Interrupt a psionic attack.
* *Psionic Removal*: Unsafely remove a psionic mutation.
* *Quick Fix*: Heal 1d6 HPS immediately.
* *Radiation Arrest*: Stop ongoing effects of radiation.
* *Radiation Cure*: Reverse all radiation effects (even HPS damage).
* *Reattach Members*: Reverse the handiwork of knites.
* *Resuscitate*: Restore to zero HPS and unconscious.
* *Restore Attribute*: Revive an injured attribute (not from aging).
* *Sentience*: Sentience present. Yes/No.
* *Stabilize*: Stop persona from losing negative HPS.
* *Stasis Bag*: Safely stuff into stasis bag.
* *Strong Fix*: Heal 2d6 HPS over 2 units.
* *Subliminal Suggestion*: Interject a thought without detection.
* *Unstun*: Immediately remove stun effect from one persona.
* *Unstun Group*: Unstun all within one hex range.
* *Vision Restoration*: Restore any blindness.
