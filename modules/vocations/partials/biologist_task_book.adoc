.*Task Descriptions*
* *Age*: Estimate chronologic age.
* *Alien organization*: Solo, family, herd, pack.
* *Biological*: Is it biological? Yes/No.
* *Cloning*: Make a copy of a biologic entity.
* *Dangerous*: Is biological organism a threat?
* *Energy Source*: What does it eat?
* *Wildlife*: Is the alien wildlife? Yes/No.
* *Gene Design*: Create new gene expressions.
* *Health Care*: Is there a healer in the group?
* *Leader Finder*: Identify leader or presence of leader.
* *Living*: Is it alive and living? Yes/No.
* *Maturity*: Estimate stage in life cycle (child, adult).
* *Mutated*: Mutated. Yes/No.
* *Mutation Type*: Psionic or phenomic.
* *Phenomic Identification*: Identify nature of phenomic mutation.
* *Psionic Identification*: Identify nature of psionic mutation.
* *Ranged Attacks*: Is this alien gonna fling stuff?
* *Sentience*: Sentience present. Yes/No.
* *Smarter than me*: Is an anthro or alien smarter than the biologist?
* *Threat level*: Degree of danger potential.
