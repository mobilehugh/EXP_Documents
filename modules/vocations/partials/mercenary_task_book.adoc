.*Task Descriptions*
* *Ambidextrous, Fling*: Use two striking weapons no penalty.
* *Ambidextrous, Grenades*: Use two grenades at once no penalty.
* *Ambidextrous, Pistols*: Use two pistols at once no penalty.
* *Ambidextrous, Rifles*: Use two rifles at once no penalty.
* *Ambidextrous, Striking*: Use two striking weapons no penalty.
* *Ambush Ambivalence*: Act during ambush as if no ambush.
* *Bolster*: Move attack bonus points to defence (DEF).
* *Breach, Civilian*: Destructively remove a civilian locked door.
* *Conscious*: Remain conscious when HPS are negative.
* *Deflank*: Remove attacker flanking bonus.
* *Divide*: Split attack bonus between attack rolls.
* *Dodge Artillery*: Half kinetic HPS damage from artillery.
* *Dodge Bomb*: Avoid bomb damage.
* *Dodge Grenade*: Half kinetic HPS damage from grenade.
* *Falling*: Do any lesser difficult task while falling.
* *Flanking*: Achieve flanking bonuses.
* *Proficiency*: Use a personal weapon as skilled.
* *Running Cover*: Run and maintain full Defence rating.
* *Safe Shot, Aerosol*: Maneuver an aerosol to avoid one target.
* *Safe Shot, Grenade*: Fling a grenade to avoid one target.
* *Salute*: Gesticulate deference to superior.
* *Snap Reload*: Reload a weapon without time loss.
* *Snap Unjam*: Unjam a weapon without time loss.
