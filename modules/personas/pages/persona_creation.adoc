= Creation

Persona creation is the most important element in any role playing game.
The personas are the "roles" that are created to allow the players to play the game.
Players typically have one persona that allows them to play the game.
Referee players have lots of personas that the players interact with.
This section gives an outline on how to create these personas.

== Families
The first step in creating a persona is determining what shape will hold that consciousness.
In EXP these shapes are organized into 4 distinct families.
These families are not created equal, and are *not* equally playable.
Some families are just like us human beans on mundane terra.
These are easy for a player to relate to and play.
Other families are omniscient, non-corporeal entities that require a referee's inside knowledge to play.
The xref:appendices:generic_mythos.adoc#_faux_taxonomy[Faux Taxonomy,window=_blank,rel="noopener noreferrer"] has even more details about these families.

It is not recommended that an inexperienced player try to role-play an insect sized alien or a tank sized combot (combat robot).
It is strongly recommended that new players create an anthro player persona.
These can be challenging enough to play with a few mutations and complex vocational task choices.
We are here to have fun after all.

.*Persona Family Choices*
* *Anthro*
** Family Hominidae
** humanoid shaped with familiar abilities
** familiar, easy to play
* *Alien*
** Family Diversidae
** randomly shaped with unique abilities
** less familiar, harder to play
* *Robot*
** Family Machinidae
** geometrically shaped with specific abilities
** least familiar, hardest to play
* *Autonomous Intelligence*
** Family Autonomidae
** no shape, exceptional abilities
** incomprehensible, impossible to play

== Players
A player persona is a character controlled by a player at the table.
The ratio is usually one persona per player.
The player is solely responsible for the actions of their persona.
This persona is how the player interacts with other players, the milieu, and referee personas.

The most common player persona is the *anthro*.
However, the rules are present for anthro, alien and robot player personas.
Not all persona types are equally easy to play.
Anthros are the most approachable.
Aliens and then robots are increasingly more difficult to play.

Each family type details it’s creation in it’s section.

There are a few elements that are common to all personas and sit outside the persona generation process.
These elements are incidentals (money, mundane equipment and artifacts), and story cues (tables creating background).
Usually player personas do not use story cues.

== Referees
A referee persona (RP) is a character controlled by the referee or any non-player.
Sometimes a referee persona will be controlled by all the players at the table.
A RP could be an anthro, alien, robot or AI.
Referee personas have varying importance to the campaign or story.
An RP could be disposable, or essential to the campaign story.
A player persona is always essential to the campaign story.
For more information about creating RPs see xref:referee_personas:a_introduction.adoc[Referee Personas, window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"].

Referee personas are player personas with a few differences.
The main difference is that their creation is guided by the referee's hand to create what the milieu or plot needs.
The referee chooses the family, vocation, genus, series or whatever is needed.
Referee persona creation also allows for entirely randomized persona creation.
Basically use the player persona data with some choices or randomly generated referee persona elements.
