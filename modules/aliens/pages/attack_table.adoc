= Alien Attack Table

The combat rules in the Roll-Playing System employs an attack matrix called the attack table.
The player refers to the attack table every time she makes an attack roll.
Following are the details of how to calculate an attack table for an alien persona.
This section has cursory explanations and no detailed calculations.
For more info jump to xref:combat:attack_table_alien.adoc[Alien Attack Table,window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"]

The attack table is composed of four roll bonuses spread across 3 attack types.

.*Attack Roll Bonuses*
* xref:combat:attack_table_details.adoc#_skilled_bonus[Skilled Bonus,window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"]
** when using natural attacks like claws, hooves or spittle
** may apply to skilled weapons if the alien is a tool user
* xref:combat:attack_table_details.adoc#_raw_bonus[Raw Bonus,window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"]
** can never apply to natural attacks
** may apply to unskilled weapons it the alien is an educated tool user
* xref:combat:attack_table_details.adoc#_max_roll[Max Roll,window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"]
** the highest roll possible
* xref:combat:attack_table_details.adoc#_force_bonus[Force Bonus,window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"]
** always zero for natural attacks, as STR is rolled into the damage roll
** may apply to weapons if the alien is a tool user

.*Attack Types*
* xref:combat:attack_types.adoc#_strike_attacks[Striking attacks,window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"]
** Mouths, claws, beaks, hooves, horns, antlers
* xref:combat:attack_types.adoc#_fling_attacks[Fling attacks,window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"]
** Quills, spittles, spines, cones, bone spurs
* xref:combat:attack_types.adoc#_shoot_attacks[Shooting attacks,window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"]
** Lazer eyes, energy bolts, gas propelled chunks

These items create a matrix of arithmetic fun called an attack table.
If the alien were attacking with her natural claw attack the player would add her skilled strike bonus to her attack roll.
These bonuses are based on the persona's attributes, increase with experience levels.

.*Aliens and Artifact Weapons*
* Flora or Fauna - No weapons use or interest in them at all, always natural attacks
* Tool Using - May use a weapon with Raw Bonus of zero
* Educated - May use Vocation Attack Table and it’s Skilled Bonus
