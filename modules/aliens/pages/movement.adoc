= Alien Movement

Movement rate is one of the most important persona attributes in the tactical combat rules.
Movement rate determines how fast an alien can move during combat.
The faster the persona can move, the better they can evade and the quicker they can get to a target point.
The movement rate, often just called ‘move,’ is measured in hexes per unit (h/u or hu).

The alien’s shape determines how she moves, *not* how fast she moves.
Denizens may hop, slither, scuttle, slime or gallop along.
The type of locomotion does not affect the alien’s movement rate.
For instance, an alien with snail leg parts would move as fast as an alien with leopard leg parts.

The alien's dexterity (DEX) determines how fast she moves.
So an alien snail and an alien leopard with the same DEX would have the same movement rate.
An alien's actual movement rate takes a bit more work to determine

== Quarter Unit
The quarter unit is a parcel of movement that the player uses to determine how fast the alien moves.
The quarter unit is determined by dividing the alien's dexterity score by four.
This value is rounded in the player's favour.
Which in this case is rounding up.
So an alien with a DEX of 12 would have a quarter unit of 3.
While an alien with a DEX of 11 would also have a quarter unit of 3.

.*Quarter Unit Calculation*
* quarter unit = DEX / 4
** quarter unit = 16 / 4 = 4
** quarter unit = 9 / 4 = 2.25 = 2

The lower the quarter unit the slower the alien.
The quarter unit determines how fast the alien runs, flies and/or swims.

== Terrain Totals
Each body part includes some lowercase letters in parenthesis.
This is the alien body part's terrain data.
For example, Goose (law) indicates goose shaped body part operates on land (l), water (w) and air (a).
While, Goat (l) indicates that the goat shaped body part only operates on land (l).

The terrain types allow aliens to run, fly, swim, be amphibious or immobile as a tree.

There are a few edge cases.
A body part of None has no terrain to total.
This will make the alien slower overall.
The terrain data of (s-l), (s-w), or (s-a) indicates a sessile body part.
This does not contribute to the alien's terrain total, but does indicate land, water or air features.
Sessile land (s-l) would be like a plant or tree.
Sessile water (s-w) would have aquatic features but not move, like an underwater plant or floating seaweed.
Sessile air (s-a) would not fly, but could float in the air.

== Calculating
The quarter unit and terrain totals are multiplied together to determine how fast any alien moves.
Let's consider an alien with the convenient DEX of 12.
This will generate a quarter unit of 3.
Using the terrain totals of the Sheep, Wasp, Duck shaped alien above we get the following

.*Calculating Alien Movement Rates*
* *Terrain Total times Quarter Unit*
** Land = 3 x 3 = 9 h/u
** Air = 2 x 3 = 6 h/u
** Water = 1 x 3 = 3 h/u

This alien can run at 9 h/u, fly at 6 h/u and swim at 3 h/u.

An alien's movement rate should not exceed its DEX score.
So an alien with a DEX of 12 should not have a land move of 14 h/u.
