= Alien Attack Rationalization

If the alien has multiple attacks the referee and player should consider rationalizing the attack types and attack damage amounts.
*This process is optional, but recommended.*
The goal is not to nerf the player’s precious alien killing machine, but to make her persona more organic and play friendly.

Most biologic creatures with multiple attacks have multiple different attacks for different situations.
The most famous would be a dragon that breathes fire, and slashes with her claws.
Cats on mundane terra can bite, scratch or completely ignore you.
It would be very unusual for a biologic creature that has multiple attacks to have just one.

== Attack Types
Attack type rationalization makes the alien persona more organic.
The goal is to blunt lethality, and create faux biologic realism.

The table is a bit daunting.
The player finds their persona's attack type (Shoot or Fling) and number of Attacks (2, 3, 4, 5, 6).
They then roll 1d8 and that becomes the alien's attack pattern.
So if the player rolls 3 on the 1d8 when they have 3 attacks and Shoot attack type their attack pattern would be Shoot, Shoot, Fling.

Strike does not appear on the table because the number of strike attacks will always equal to the number of attacks.
So an alien with 4 attacks and Strike attack type would have 4 strike attacks per unit.

== Attack Damage
Attack damage can also be rationalized for aliens.
This allows the persona to have a bite attack at 1d12 and a fling attack at 1d10.
The step down for damage die rolls is based on best to worst attack type.
However, the referee and player can use any pattern that fits the milieu and story.

To rationalize attack damage the player looks up the step down set for their STR and size category.
So a Large sized alien with a STR of 13 would reveal a set of 1) 3d10, 2) 1d20 and 3) 1d12.
The persona's favourite attack takes the highest value.
So if the alien had two attack types Fling and Strike they could choose fling 3d10 and strike 1d20.
Only if they have 3 attack __types__ would they use all 3.
Note that this is number of attack types, *not* number of attacks.
So if an alien had 4 attacks and only 1 attack type they would use the same damage for each attack.
