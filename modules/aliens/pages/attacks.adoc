= Alien Attacks

Alien attacks are naturally evolved and built right into their being.
Alien's with advanced societies may have technological weapons, but the attacks described here are natural abilities.
The literal description of the attack type is up to the player, but the game structure of the attack is built in this section.

== Attack Sentence
After many rounds of alien arena it was determined that making an “attack sentence” was very helpful.
The combination of attacks determined about is shortened into a convenient sentence.
For example, "2 shoot for 3d10", or "2 fling for 1d20 and 1 strike for 1d12".

== Attack Rationalization
Sometimes it doesn't make sense for an alien to only have one attack type multiple times.
Presently an alien with multiple attacks would only have one attack type and one damage amount.
A dragon like alien may breath fire to attack 4 times a unit.
It may be more fun if that dragon like alien breathed fire once for a lot of damage, and slapped with tentacles for less damage.
Attack rationalization can help with this dilemma.
