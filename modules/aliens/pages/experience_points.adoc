= Alien Vocation

The default vocation for an alien is alien.
While it is possible for an alien to follow a career path found in xref:vocations:a_introduction.adoc[vocations] this is rare indeed.
Most aliens are just living their best alien lives.

An alien persona without a career vocation can still improve themselves and benefit from collecting experience.
Experience points (EXPS) are the currency of growth for EXP personas.
The more EXPS a persona collects, the better their persona gets.
For a more detailed review of experience points jump to xref:uncombat:experience.adoc[Experience,window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"].

== Experience Points
Aliens earn full experience points for combat when using their natural abilities.
Aliens earn partial combat experience when using a weapon other than their natural abilities.
Aliens only receive 25% of combat experience when using weapons.
Aliens can earn EXPS for role-playing just like any other persona.

=== Roll Benefits
An alien accrues many of the benefits that a persona earns when her experience levels increase.
For example, her attack bonuses improve, her adjusted attribute rolls improve, and her saving throws improve.

=== Society Benefits
At each new EXPS level, the player can return to the alien society section and recheck to see if her persona alien can personally evolve.
For example, an alien with only language can check again for culture.
The persona would not check for religion, although it would be fun to burden a player persona with an unplanned religious hankering.
The persona can advance to vocations if the dice allow.
At this point, the player can choose to follow a vocation abandoning her species-based EXPS collection.

== Career Vocations
If an alien has a true career vocation, like a mechanic or a mercenary, they advance in experience levels as that vocation.
So an alien mechanic would use the mechanic experience point table.
