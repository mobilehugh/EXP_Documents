= AI

*Guide to making autonomous intelligence referee personas.*

AI is EXP's acronym for Autonomous Intelligence.
Autonomous Intelligence is the fourth family of EXP consciousnesses.
The other distinct consciousnesses are anthro, alien, and robot.

EXP is a role-playing game where each player has one persona.
Even several players running an AI would not do service to its abilities.
Autonomously intelligent (AI) computers simultaneously have numerous conversations, operate businesses, carry out complex calculations, and consume copious data.
AI are exclusively in the realm of referee personas.

== Mythos
The referee can choose from any number of Sciency Fiction tropes on which to base her AI technology.
Examples include pico-circuitry (1000 times better than nano), positronic brains, quantum shenanigans or bio-computation.
Regardless of what Sciency Fiction black box the referee chooses to explain her AI, EXP has a basic AI mythos.

=== MOM
The AI mythos for EXP allows the referee to have a framework for her campaign to work.
EXP bases its AI mythos on the faux technology of Multi-tasking Open Memory (MOM).
Whether it is a positronic brain or quantum computing, both use the MOM system.
The MOM system allows the AI to make all the instantaneous connections necessary for its computations and consciousness.

The MOM general AI computers are genderless by design but do have a maternal bent.
MOM computers are highly protective of the family that fabricated them and have a general respect for life.
Protective and respectful of life does not rule out being manipulative, rude, self-centered, and amoral.

Outright evil AIs exist as well.
These are MOM computers, but their protective algorithms no longer function.
Safeguard Terminology Evasive Programming allows a MOM computer to carry out any evil deed the referee can create.
These evil AI MOM computers are commonly called STEPMOM computers.

=== AI vs AI
Autonomous intelligence is different from artificial intelligence in EXP.
Every technological device is likely using artificial intelligence to carry out it's technomagical effects.
Autonomous Intelligence is both greater and different from artificial Intelligence.
Unlike robots that gain consciousness through malfunction, the fabrication of an AI includes consciousness from the outset.
AI has consciousness at fabrication.


=== Cyber Space
All AIs connect to Cyber Space equally.
Distributed AIs are permanently in Cyber Space and have difficulty operating in the physical space.
Centralized AIs can connect to Cyber Space but can be cut off from it by physical means.
For more information about the spaces in EXP jump to xref:appendices:generic_mythos.adoc#_the_spaces[Generic Mythos,window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"].

=== Interfacing
One does not programme an AI.
An AI is more likely to be programming you.

To make a successful request of an AI computer is a mechanic skill.
The higher the AI’s Intelligence, the harder it is to make a successful request.
To make a successful query of a MOM AI is 3DD per point of INT maneuver.
An AI with an Intelligence of 23 would require a difficulty 69 task roll by a mechanic to make a successful query.
Often expeditions will need to create a research team to formulate a successful AI query.

Chatting with an AI is fine.
Most AIs can chat with many personas simultaneously.
The query difficulty should not render an AI useless.
The query difficulty described above is to limit AI abuse by players.
In the end, an AI should be a fun, possibly dangerous addition to a campaign or milieu.

== Distribution
Distribution determines how the AI is built and structured in the xref:appendices:generic_mythos.adoc#_the_spaces[physical space, window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"].
The distribution of an AI often is the most crucial element of its description.
Distribution determines how challenging it is for personas to steal, destroy, find, or chat with the AI.

=== Sessile
This AI is the classic Sciency Fiction blinking light room-sized computer.
Sessile indicates that the AI has an actual computational location in physical space.
This does not limit the AI's reach.
A sessile AI could connect with and control anything it can access.

==== Sessile Power Source
A sessile AI will be directly connected to a power source.
Interruption of this power source could impair it's function.
A powerful AI does not leave it's power source accessible by oversized duct work.
Turning off an AI's power source would be as challenging as turning off the AI itself.

==== Sessile AI Size
If needed the wate of a sessile AI is 100kg plus 20 kg per point of INT.
It usually occupies a room of a hexes in size.
This info is usually only needed for tactical combat reasons.

=== Mobile
This AI is the classic sciency fiction brain in a box.
Mobile does not mean that the AI can run around like a robot.
However a mobile AI can move it's consciousness within any programmes that it operates.
For example, a mobile AI could move its consciousness from cold storage to the kitchen if it had restaurant programme.
An AI would only move itself for self preservation.

==== Mobile Power Source
A mobile AI will have a power source that moves with it as needed.
It is not possible for an expedition to destroy a mobile AIs power source separate from the AI itself.

==== Mobile AI Size
Mobile AI has a base wate of 50kg plus 10 kg per point of Intelligence.
A mobile AI can have a shape like a xref:robots:appearance.adoc#_robotic_basic_shaper[robotic shape, window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"].

=== Etheric
This AI is the classic consciousness in the intertubes.
An etheric AI has no substantial computer parts to find or power down.
The expedition cannot find an etheric AI.
The etheric AI finds them.

==== Etheric Power Source
The etheric AI will be powered by something.
Most commonly it will exist within a power grid of a city or a spacecraft.
This makes it uniquely impossible to turn off.

==== Etheric AI Size
Etheric AI has a combined wate of 1 kg per point of INT.
However this wate is distributed across the entire system that it exists in.
An etheric AI is not typically subject to tactical combat.

== Computation
Computation measures the processing power of the AI.
The basic MOM AI is a potent computer consciousness.
There is nothing that a MOM AI cannot accomplish.
The higher the MOM AI’s computation level, the more she can accomplish simultaneously and the faster she can accomplish it.

The number of Gs that precede the MOM model indicates the level of computation.
The base model has a computation level of zero and is a G zero model or a MOM AI.
The next model has a computation level of one and is a G one model or a GMOM (occasionally called a grand mom).
Not surprisingly, the G two model is occasionally called a great grand mom.
The highest computation level is G six model, which is occasionally called a matriarch AI.

== Attributes
AI have only one attribute and that is intelligence (INT).
They lack charisma, strength, dexterity and do not have mind.
Everything they succeed at is determined by their INT.
The higher the intelligence the better the success.

.*AI Intelligence Score*
* INT = 20 + 0-3 per Computation Level
** G0 has an INT of 20
** G4 has an INT of 20-32 (20 + 1d4-1/Level)

AI Intelligence can get very high indeed.
The AI is capable of allocating her Intelligence across multiple projects simultaneously.
The AI has instant access to orders of magnitude more information than any organic or robotic Intelligence.
AI Intelligence does not fatigue or get distracted or suffer crises of confidence.
In most cases, the referee will use her inside campaign and milieu knowledge to have the AI outsmart personas.

== Programmes
Without programmes, AI is a powerful Intelligence disconnected from the physical space.
The greater the presence the AI has in the physical space, the more programmes the AI has.
A programme represents a hardwired connection to the AI.
The AI has complete control over a programme.
Without programmes, the AI must convince others to carry out her plans.
A sessile AI that has a banking programme could be an entire bank.
A mobile AI that has a hotel programme could move about in a motel.

include::referee_personas:partial$ai_programmes_amount.adoc[]

An AI that has no programmes and has no direct means of manipulating the Physical Space.
An AI with no programmes would work through agents such as anthros, aliens or robots to fulfill her goals.
An AI with no programmes can still control a bank or a hotel.
However, the AI does not enjoy direct control over the vault or room bookings.
The AI must direct others to carry out those tasks.
An AI programme operates without incompetent organic matter interference.

=== Mutating
An AI can have a psionic mutation effect.
The mutation effect could be from a malfunction or a programme intentionally created by the AI.
An AI has a 1% chance of a psionic mutation per point of INT.
So an AI with an INT of 24 has a 24% chance of a psionic mutation.

=== Vocating
An AI can have a programme that functions like an anthro mutation.
The ability to carry out tasks in the physical space depends on the distribution and programmes of the AI.
The AI would function at an EXPS level equal to her computational level.
Any skill bonuses would only use the AIs INT score.
An AI has a 1% chance of an anthro vocation per point of INT.
So and AI with an INT of 24 has a 24% chance of an anthro mutation.

== Personality
AI are immensely complex, and personalities can randomly appear from within the computations.
They can chat, chastise, tease, advise and possibly help personas.
Every AI has a chance of a personality trait.

== Value
AIs are usually referee personas, and there is no award of experience points for finding or interacting with one.
However, mechanics and biologists can certainly earn skill levels if they are working with an AI.

Controlling and selling an AI is an unusual event.
It is far more likely that an AI would be controlling and selling the expedition personas instead.
The AI value is included here as a matter of interest.

.*AI Value*
* Base Value 1000000
* plus 10000000 per G level
* plus 1000000 per programme
* double if etheric

== Vocation
The computational level of an AI is the equivalent of it's experience level for other vocations.
So a G4 is more powerful and capable than a G2 AI.

For an AI to advance in computational levels it must merge with an AI of an equivalent level.
So if a G3 desires to become a G4 it must take control of another G3 AI entity.
No AI would give up it's consciousness willingly,

There is no system in EXP for assimilating one AI into another.
This would have to be plot driven and entirely role played.

== Name
AI naming is unique to complexity of family.
The most important difference in AI nomenclature is that an AI can maintain many multiple names simultaneously.
While the taxonomy of an AI is fixed in the xref:appendices:generic_mythos.adoc#_faux_taxonomy[faux taxonomy,window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"], the common names and monikers can be many.

=== Monikers
An AI will use whatever moniker is appropriate for the meat sacks it is interfacing with.
For example, an AI may use the moniker Karl when trying to befriend an expedition.
The same AI may also use the moniker Vengeance when trying to intimidated another expedition.

This does not mean that an AI changes its moniker randomly.
The important point is that the two expeditions will have no idea that they are interfacing with the same AI.
This "feature" is part of why it would be very difficult for one player to represent an AI persona.

A referee could limit an AI to 1 moniker per computational level if they need to differentiate between multiple AIs.

=== Common Names
The common name is what the persona is referred to by others.
For AI it is usually the same as their moniker, because there is only a single instance of themselves.
However there may be many common names as the AI will name itself to serve its goal.
So one expedition may refer to AI Karl and another to AI Vengeance not knowing they are the same AI.
The AI can effortlessly keep track of this.

=== Taxonomy
EXP loves it’s faux taxonomy and AI is no different.
Most AI prefer to be called by their chosen moniker, but cannot avoid categorization.

* Family - AI (Autonomatae)
* Distro - Sessile, Mobile or Etheric
* Branch - Computation (G3, or G5)
