= Robots

*Guide to making anthro referee personas.*

Robots are inorganic lifeforms.
In the faux taxonomy, robots are of the family machinidae
Machinidae are life forms that are inorganic and fabricated.
Anthros, Aliens or other Robots can fabricate robots.
The robots here are referee persona robots fabricated by the referee.

== Robot Fabrication
The core anthro is created using the player persona xref:robots:a_introduction.adoc[Robot Fabrication, window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"] section.
The referee can make choices that reflect story needs right there, or use the variations described below.

== Deviations
These are strange things that referees may want in their referee personas that don't really work for player personas.
This includes extremities of size and age.
Or specific descriptions that the referee desires like strong, fast or ugly.

=== Size
These extreme sizes are nano and colossal

==== Nano
Any robot that has a wate of 4.2 kg or less has a chance of becoming an extremely small nano bot.
The chance of the robot becoming a nano bot is 1 in 6 (1d6)
Nano bots have a wate of  0 to 999 grams (1d1000 - 1).
A nano bot cannot have a wate greater than a kilogram.
Nano robots have no change to their attributes, abilities or combat values.

==== Colossal
Any robot with a wate of 4200 kg or more has a chance of becoming a colossal machine.
The chance of the robot becoming a colossal machine is 1 in 6 (1d6).
Colossal robots have a wate of 6 to 600 tons (1d100 times 6).
Colossal robots have no change to their attributes, abilities or combat values.
However, in regards to ramming, colossal machines can increase their ramming ratio to 12 (versus 4).
Colossal robots were formerly referred to as Mighty Machines.
