= Robot Attributes

Robot persona attribute values are equivalent to organic persona attribute values.
A robot’s Strength of 10 is the same as an anthro’s Strength of 10.
However, the pathway to get to those equivalent values is convoluted.
Players generate prime values for the 4 robotic prime attributes.
These attributes determine series selection and inform regular attribute scores.
The robot MND attribute works differently than for organics and they have

== Prime Attributes
Prime attributes are the robot persona’s most important attributes.
The prime attributes determine the persona’s robot type and indicate each attribute score’s range.
The prime attributes are Constitution, Dexterity, Intelligence and Strength.

Each prime attribute has a value between 1 and 4 (1d4).
The higher the prime score, the higher the final attribute score will be.
The player records the prime attribute score on the robot persona record.
The prime scores are immutable, and while the robot’s attribute score deteriorates, her prime score does not.

There is a location on the robot persona record sheet to record the robot's prime attributes.

== Regular Attributes
The player generates these regular attributes the same as she would for any other persona.
She rolls the assigned dice and records the score on her persona record sheet.
The regular attributes are Awareness (AWE), Charisma (CHA) and social standing (SOC).
Constitution, Intelligence, Dexterity and Strength are determined by their prime values.

=== Mind (MND)
Robot personas have a Mind score of zero.
A zero Mind does not indicate that robots are psionically vulnerable and easily manipulated.
Robots do not have Mind because their consciousness does not exist within the Think Space.
Robots are unaffected by mental attacks that function through the xref:appendices:generic_mythos.adoc#_the_spaces[Think Space, window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"].
Robots cannot be mind-controlled or subject to telempathy or have their mind read.
If a robot must test her MND, she will use her INT score instead.

== Series Attributes
Series attributes are dependent on the series and model of the robot.
At this point the robot model and series have not been determined.

=== Adaptability (ADP)
The second irregular attribute is adaptability (ADP).
Robotic Adaptability determines how easily the robot can be altered.
Some robots are very amenable to being altered, and have a high adaptability.
For example a Hobbot has a very high adaptability, while a heavy offensive combot has a very low adaptability.

=== Hit Points
Each robot type has hit points listed on its fabrication info sheet.
The player calculates her robot persona’s hit points max when the robot type is determined.
The player multiplies the robot’s Constitution score by the hit points listed on the fabrication info sheet.
An explorations robot (E) with a 20 CON could have between 220 and 320 hit points max.
A datalyzer (D) with a 4 CON could have between 4 and 12 hit points max.
The robot world is entirely unfair.

== Example

=== Primes
The player rolls 1d4 for her CON_P, DEX_P, INT_P and STR_P.
These are called constitution prime, dexterity prime, intelligence prime and strength prime.

.*Prime Attributes*
* CON_P = 1
* DEX_P = 2
* INT_P = 3
* STR_P = 4

These are recorded on the persona record and will help determined the robot model and series.

=== Regulars
The player first rolls her regular attributes just like any other persona being generated.
She rolls 3d6 for her Awareness and Charisma and a 1d1000 for her social standing.
Her persona’s regular attributes scores are 11 AWE, 8 CHA and 653 SOC.
She records these scores on her persona record.
Remember that Mind (MND) is automatically 0 for a robot persona.

The remaining four regular attributes CON, DEX, INT, and STR need to be translated from the prime scores.
Based on her prime attributes, she rolls 1d8 for her CON, 1d6 + 7 for her DEX, 1d6 + 13 for her INT and 1d10 + 19 for her STR.
The player rolls a CON of 6, a DEX or 11, an INT of 18 and a STR of 27.
These are the attributes used to interact with the world.

=== Series
Series attributes are dependent on the series and model that the player selects for her robot.
In the case of a xref:robots:series_hobbot.adoc[Hobbot,window=_blank,rel="noopener noreferrer"] robot persona:
her Adaptability (ADP) is set at 42, and her Hit Points would be 2d4 per point of CON.
Since her CON (not CON_P) is 6 should would roll 12d4 to determine Hit Points Max.
The player rolls 24 for her hit points.

== Alternate Method
Attribute generation is intimately woven into robot model and series selection.
If this Byzantine anachronistic process is too much one can just straight to xref:robots:series__selection_skip.adoc[Robot Series Selection, window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer" ].
This will allow the player to pick their series and model and generate the attributes at that time.
