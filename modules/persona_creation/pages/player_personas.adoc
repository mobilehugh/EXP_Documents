= Player Personas

[quote]
____
Be what you want to be.
____

A player persona is a character controlled by a player at the table.
The ratio is usually one persona per player.
The player is solely responsible for the actions of their persona.
This persona is how the player interacts with other players, the milieu, and referee personas.

The most common player persona is the anthro.
However, the rules are present for anthro, alien and robot player personas.
Anthros, aliens and robots are organized in order of how difficulty they are to play.
An AI cannot be run as a player persona, but players may collaborate to help play an AI persona.

Referee personas can be disposable to the story and can be limited in documentation.
Player personas are the focus of the story, and usually are well documented.

.*Player Persona Choices*
* Anthro (Hominidae) - humanoids based on mundane terran creatures, with similar abilities
** Genus (Homo Avis, Homo Canis, Homo Felis, etc)
** Species (specific appearance - Hawk, Polar Bear, Generis, etc)
* Alien (Diversidae) - randomly shaped creatures with random strange abilities
** Genus (Torso faux name)
** Species (Head faux name)
* Robot (Machinidae) - mechanically shaped entities with specific abilities
** Series (Combot, Datalyzer, etc)
** Model (Expendable, Exatmo, Industrial, etc)

NOTE: Players will almost always be anthros.

== Creation
Each family type details it's creation in it's section.
A player should be able to create a player persona by step wise following
Each section has enough information to create an overly detailed persona for the Roll Playing System
There are a few elements that are common to all personas and sit outside the persona generation process.
These elements are incidentals (money, mundane equipment and artifacts), and story cues (tables creating background).
Usually player personas do not use story cues.

.*Player Persona Creation*
. Choose and create core persona
.. xref:anthros:a_introduction.adoc[Anthro Delivery, window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"] - most familiar, easiest to play
.. xref:aliens:a_introduction.adoc[Alien Evolution, window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"] - less familiar, harder to play
.. xref:robots:a_introduction.adoc[Robot Fabrication, window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"] - totally unfamiliar, hardest to play
. Equip with xref:persona_creation:incidentals.adoc[Incidentals, window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"] - money, equipment, toys
. Embellish with xref:persona_creation:rp_story_cues.adoc[Story Cues, window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"]
. Record on a xref:appendices:printables.adoc[Persona Record Sheet, window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"]
. Have a lot of fun!
