== Player Personas
A player persona is a character controlled by a player at the table.
The ratio is usually one persona per player.
The player is solely responsible for the actions of their persona.
This persona is how the player interacts with other players, the milieu, and referee personas.

.*Player Persona Choices*
* Anthro
** familiar, easy to play
* Alien
** less familiar, harder to play
* Robot
** least familiar, hardest to play
* AI
** incomprehensible, referee only

The most common player persona is the anthro.
However, the rules are present for anthro, alien and robot player personas.
Not all persona types are equally easy to play.
Anthros are the most approachable.
Aliens and then robots are increasingly more difficult to play.

Each family type details it’s creation in it’s section.
A player should be able to create a player persona by step wise following
Each section has enough information to create an overly detailed persona for the Roll Playing System
There are a few elements that are common to all personas and sit outside the persona generation process.
These elements are incidentals (money, mundane equipment and artifacts), and story cues (tables creating background).
Usually player personas do not use story cues.

== Referee Personas
A referee persona (RP) is a character controlled by the referee or any non-player.
Sometimes a referee persona will be controlled by all the players at the table.
A RP could be an anthro, alien, robot or AI.
Referee personas have varying importance to the campaign or story.
An RP could be disposable, or essential to the campaign story.
A player persona is always essential to the campaign story.
For more information about creating RPs see xref:persona_creation:referee_personas.adoc[Referee Personas, window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"].

=== Creation
Each family type has it’s own creation pathway.
RP creation is very stripped down and has options that are not available to player personas.
An understanding of player persona creation is helpful for referee persona creation.
There are a few elements that are common to all personas and sit outside the RP generation process.
These elements are incidentals (money, mundane equipment and artifacts), and story cues (tables creating background).
RPs are highly dependent on story cues.
