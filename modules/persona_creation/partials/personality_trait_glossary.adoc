.*Personality Trait Glossary*
Acrophobia::
Fear of heights.

Aesthetic::
Appreciates good taste.

Agoraphobia::
Fear of open spaces.

Aichmophobia::
Fear of sharp objects, points or blades.

Ailurophobia::
Fear of small furry creatures.

Aloof::
Shows little interest in company.

Altruistic::
Concerned with the welfare of others.

Amnesia::
Gaps in memory.

Amoral::
No consideration of right or wrong.

Anthrophobia::
Fear of anthros and humanoids.

Anxiety::
Apprehension without an obvious threat.

Arrogant::
Overestimates her abilities dismisses others.

Astrophobia::
Fear of outer space.

Avaricious::
Hoards wealth and resists spending or sharing it.

Barbaric::
Crude or wild in manner.

Bibliophobia::
Fear of books.

Callous::
No concern for the suffering of others.

Capricious::
Changes plans on a whim.

Careless::
Pays little attention to details..

Cautious::
Considers risks before acting.

Cheerful::
Upbeat and ready to find enjoyment.

Claustrophobia::
Fear of confined spaces.

Compassionate::
Recognizes suffering.

Compuphobia::
Fear of computers.

Confabulation::
Supplies invented accounts or recollections and treats them as true.

Contrary::
Habitually opposes everything.

Covetous::
Desires other peoples stuff.

Cowardly::
Avoids danger and pain.

Craven::
Avoids danger and pain

Curious::
Wants to explore unfamiliar things.

Deceitful::
Misleads others through lies.

Diplomatic::
Communicates tactfully.

Dipsomania::
An overwhelming desire to consume beverages.

Dressy::
Over importance of clothing.

Driven::
Goal determined, and unwilling to give up.

Easy Going::
Relaxed, adaptable and rarely upset by minor difficulties.

Egotistical::
Exaggerates her own importance.

Ergophobia::
Fear of work.

Even-Tempered::
Maintains a calm, steady disposition.

Exacting::
Demands precision, care and high standards.

Fanatical::
Pursues a cause or belief with excessive zeal.

Fearless::
Faces danger without fear.

Forgiving::
Readily pardons wrongdoing.

Friendly::
Welcomes company and readily builds rapport.

Gonnephobia::
Fear of guns.

Haughty::
Acts superior and treats with disdain.

Hedonistic::
Makes personal enjoyment a central pursuit.

Hot Tempered::
Becomes angry quickly.

Retiring::
Modest about her achievements.

Hydrophobia::
Fear of water or liquids.

Hypochondria::
Persistent fear of illness.

Hysteria::
Overwhelming emotional excitement.

Iconoclastic::
Challenges revered beliefs and authority.

Immoral::
Knowingly acts against the moral standards.

Imposing::
Commanding or intimidating presence.

Inquisitive::
Asks questions persistently.

Insensitive::
Fails to notice or consider others.

Irreverent::
Shows little respect for customs, or authority.

Jealous::
Fears losing valued affection.

Jornemania::
A conviction that safety awaits at the end of a journey.

Kindly::
Gentle, considerate and helpful in everyday dealings.

Kleptomania::
A compulsion to steal.

Liar::
Habitually offers falsehoods in place of the truth.

Linonophobia::
Fear of rope or string.

Loquacious::
Talks readily and at length.

Lustful::
Strongly preoccupied with romantic desire.

Malevolent::
Wishes harm or misfortune on others.

Martyr::
Readily sacrifices her own comfort.

Mechanomania::
Believing technology provides the best solution always.

Megalomania::
An exaggerated sense of power and entitlement.

Melancholia::
Persistent, profound sadness and gloom.

Mischievous::
Enjoys minor trouble and misbehaviour.

Monomania::
A fixation on thing that crowds out other concerns.

Moody::
Changes mood frequently and unpredictably.

Morose::
Sullen, gloomy and inclined to withdraw from company.

Mysophobia::
Fear of dirt or contamination.

Necrophobia::
Fear of death or dead things.

Nictophobia::
Fear of darkness or night.

Obsequious::
Uses excessive flattery to gain approval.

Obsessive::
Returns repeatedly to an idea or activity.

Opinionated::
Holds strong views and readily expresses them.

Opthalmophobia::
Fear of being stared at.

Optimistic::
Habitually expects a favourable outcome.

Paranoia::
Excessive suspicion that others intend harm.

Perverted::
Habitually defies accepted moral conventions.

Pessimistic::
Habitually expects a bad outcome.

Phobophobia::
Fear of becoming afraid or experiencing fear.

Pious::
Devoted to religious beliefs and duties.

Ponderer::
Spends excessive time considering questions.

Ponderous::
Slow, and heavy in thought or expression.

Practicophobia::
Fear of vocations or professions.

Prankster::
Habitually plays tricks and practical jokes.

Proud::
Takes satisfaction in her achievements.

Pyromania::
A compelling desire to start fires.

Religiosity::
Interprets events with religious explanations.

Robophobia::
Fear of robots.

Sadistic::
Takes pleasure in cruelty or the suffering of others.

Scheming::
Habitually plots to manipulate people or events toward a desired outcome.

Scrupulous::
Carefully follows moral principles, duties or agreed rules.

Secretive::
Conceals personal information, intentions or plans.

Sensitive::
Readily notices and reacts to emotions.

Slothful::
Avoids effort and prefers inactivity.

Sober::
Serious, restrained in judgement and behaviour.

Studious::
Devoted to learning, observation and research.

Suspicious::
Questions the motives of others.

Taciturn::
Speaks little or prefers solitude.

Technophobia::
Fear of technology.

Thrifty::
Conserves resources and avoids waste.

Trusting::
Readily accepts the honesty and good intentions of others.

Truthful::
Reports what she believes to be true.

Unforgiving::
Holds onto resentment and refuses to pardon wrongdoing.

Vengeful::
Seeks retaliation for real or perceived wrongs.

Violent::
Readily uses physical force to resolve conflicts.

Wasteful::
Squanders resources, opportunities or possessions.

Xenophobia::
Fear of strangers or unfamiliar peoples.

Zoophobia::
Fear of animals or aliens.
