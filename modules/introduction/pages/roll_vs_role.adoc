= Roll-Playing vs Role-Playing

[quote]
____
*A tale of two systems*
____

EXP has two play-tested sets of rules.
The tactical combat rules form the oldest ruleset.
The tactical combat rules work best with hex paper and miniatures and use a 1d1000 for attack rolls.
The tactical combat rules are detailed and dice-heavy, and they form the basis for the technology and mutation sections’ details.

The theatrical combat rules form the next oldest ruleset.
The theatrical combat rules are a simplified version of the tactical combat rules.
The theatrical combat rules simplify the tactical combat rules by using simpler dice.
The theatrical system works with hundred-sided, twenty-sided, ten-sided and six-sided dice.
The theatrical system sacrifices detail and subtlety for speed and fun.
The theatrical combat rules now have their own dice mechanic, but dividing down the kilodie is still an option.

== Roll-Playing System
The Roll-Playing System uses the tactical combat rules and the xref:combat:kilo_die_mechanic.adoc[Kilodie Mechanic, window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"].
This ruleset works best on hex paper using miniatures and would enjoy integration with a virtual tabletop.
The Roll-Playing System depends on the roll of the dice.
The dice direct most of the outcomes.
Players need to do a lot of arithmetic and reading.
Onboarding of new players requires patience and supportive teaching.

.*Roll-Playing System is for you if...*
* You have played previous RPGs
* You like to roll dice
* You are not afraid of maths
* You like details

The Roll-Playing System is the default system of EXP.

== Role-Playing System
The Role-Playing System uses the theatrical combat rules and the xref:role_playing_system:four_dee_two_mechanic.adoc[4D2 Mechanic, window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"].
The rule system does not require miniatures or hex paper but works fine with both.
The Role-Playing System does not depend on the dice for decisions.
The players specifically describe the outcomes in this ruleset.
Players consult few tables and need only simple arithmetic.
The most complicated arithmetic is adding and subtracting single-digit numbers.
The persona record sheet contains words and almost no numbers.
It only takes a few minutes to create a persona, and the game mechanics are intuitive.
Onboarding of new players is immediate.

.*Role-Playing System is for you if...*
* You have played board games and can roll six-sided dice
* You are dice averse and would rather talk
* You are not fond of remembering numbers
* You don’t care too much about details

You can find the Role-Playing System in the xref:role_playing_system:a_introduction.adoc[appendix, window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"].
