== What is this?
EXP: The Game of Technological Chaos is a paper and dice tabletop Sciency Fiction role-playing game (RPG).
EXP is meant to be played with real people around a real table.
Everything you need to play is on this website right here. xref:introduction:a_introduction.adoc[Let's go!,window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"]

* Create a persona from three main xref:roll_playing_system:families.adoc[families]: anthro, robot, or alien .
* Extend your persona with xref:wetware:a_introduction.adoc#_phenomics[phenomic] and xref:wetware:a_introduction.adoc#_psionics[psionic] mutations.
* Follow a xref:vocations:a_introduction.adoc[vocation] like mechanic, biologist, mercenary, or more.
* Equip your person with Sciency Fiction xref:hardware:a_introduction.adoc[hardware] like aerosols, pharma, biomech, or spacecraft.
* Play in a world of your own creation, or jump right into the xref:appendices:generic_mythos.adoc[generic mythos].

== Nerdy Details
* EXP is a heavily play tested, rigorously edited, RPG past it's 7th version.
* EXP has a history that stretches from a 1970s homebrew to yesterday's git push.
* Settle combat with a tactical war game like system or a story based theatrical one.
** the tactical system uses a xref:roll_playing_system:kilo_die_mechanic.adoc[kilodie mechanic], to allow for highly granular combat tuning.
** the theatrical system uses a xref:role_playing_system:four_dee_two_mechanic.adoc[4D2 mechanic], to create descriptive narrative outcomes.
* Randomly generate fleshed out personas with dice rolls galore, or not.
* Personas, vocations, combat, toys and mythos are integrated.
* The ruleset is resilient and rules can be shed without collapsing playability.
